1. Feeding Guidelines & Food Categories

Feed worms based on microbial activity. Always balance high-nitrogen green scraps with high-carbon brown bedding.

High-Value Trapping Lures (For 40L Central Tote)

  • Papaya & Melon Rinds: High volatile sugar content, rapid microbial breakdown.
  • Crushed Butternut & Squash: Soft, moist pulp that worms swarm within 48 hours.
  • Sweet Potato Skins & Spent Coffee Grounds: Nitrogen boost that attracts hungry juveniles.

Bulk Wedge Food (Weekly Routine)

  • Pre-Composted Garden Scraps: Partially decomposed leaves, crushed vegetable peels, soft fruit.
  • Cardboard & Paper: Shredded unprinted cardboard, egg cartons, toilet paper rolls (carbon buffer).

Strictly Do Not Feed

  • Citrus & Pineapple: High acid and d-limonene burn worm skin.
  • Onions, Garlic, Chili: Pungent compounds drive worms out of the tub.
  • Dairy, Meat, Oils: Triggers foul anaerobic odors and attracts blowflies/rodents.

2. Calcium, Grit & pH Buffering

Worms have no teeth and rely on a muscular gizzard to grind food particles.

  • Pulverized Eggshells: Bake dried eggshells and blend into fine dust. Sprinkle a tablespoon over every weekly feed. Provides essential calcium carbonate to buffer acidic food breakdown and gives necessary gizzard grit.
  • Agricultural Lime / Rock Dust: If eggshells are unavailable, a light dusting of agricultural lime (calcium carbonate—never hydrated lime!) prevents "sour crop" (protein poisoning).

3. Worm Tea vs. Raw Leachate

  • Raw Leachate (Tub Plughole Runoff): This is excess liquid draining through un-processed waste. It contains anaerobic bacteria and phytotoxins. Do not apply directly to delicate edible crops. Dilute 1:20 for non-edible garden shrubs or lawn.
  • Aerated Compost Tea (ACT - The Good Stuff): Steep 1kg of pure castings in a 10L bucket of unchlorinated well water with 1 tbsp molasses. Aerate with an aquarium air pump for 24 hours. Sprayed on leaves or roots, ACT introduces millions of beneficial aerobic microbes that suppress soil fungal pathogens.

4. Deep Cleaning & Major Maintenance

  • Clearing the Plughole: Every 6 months, inspect the plughole. Use a hose to flush fine silt out of the 19mm stone layer to maintain zero-resistance drainage.
  • Shade Cloth Inspection: Check the mesh barrier during full tub resets. Ensure no sharp twigs have torn the cloth, which could allow fine castings into the drainage stones.
  • Annual Reset: Once a year, empty both tubs down to the stone layer, rinse the 19mm aggregate with fresh well water, relight the shade cloth, and rebuild the base coir floor.
